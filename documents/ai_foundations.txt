Artificial Intelligence is built upon neural networks, which are inspired
by the biological structure of the human brain. A key component is the 
"Transformer" architecture, introduced in the 2017 paper "Attention Is All 
You Need." Unlike older Recurrent Neural Networks (RNNs), Transformers use a 
"Self-Attention" mechanism. This allows the model to weigh the importance of 
different words in a sentence simultaneously, regardless of their distance from
 each other. This parallelism significantly improved training efficiency and led to the development 
 of Large Language Models (LLMs) like GPT-4 and Claude.
